## What it is

Love My Closet keeps track of what you own, lets you plan outfits on a calendar, and nudges you back toward the pieces you forgot you had. Its own tagline is *Made to be Loved Again*, which is about the clothes, and also a little bit about the app.

Built with Flutter and Dart.

## Why it is here

It has a brand. Not a color picked at the end, an actual identity, from the square to the onboarding line that asks whether *nothing to wear* is your daily problem. Most first Flutter apps look like the Flutter starter with the serial numbers filed off. This one looks like somebody meant it.
