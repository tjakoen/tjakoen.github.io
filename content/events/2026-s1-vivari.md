## What it is

The name comes from *vivarium*, Latin for a place of life. Vivari tracks several aquariums at once: their inhabitants, water readings over time, and the care tasks that keep everything in them alive.

Built with Flutter and Dart.

## Why it is here

Nobody else in either section picked a problem this specific, and specific is the whole game. It is a hobby I know nothing about and I still understood every screen, which is the best thing I can say about a dashboard.
