*A final project from Full-stack JavaScript, 2026 first semester. Part of [Fifteen full-stack apps from a semester I almost did not survive](/calendar/2026-s1-apsi-students).*

## What it is

Roomy is a personal room inventory: record your belongings, remember where they are stored, and sketch a rough 2D layout when you rearrange or redecorate.

Built with React and Supabase.

## Why it is here

Friendly branding, a working layout planner, tests, and a status note in the README that says exactly what was verified and what was not. That last part is the one I would hire for.
