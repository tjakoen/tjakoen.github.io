*A final project from Full-stack JavaScript, 2026 first semester. Part of [Fifteen full-stack apps from a semester I almost did not survive](/calendar/2026-s1-apsi-students).*

## What it is

Clients browse classes, choose a reformer, buy packages and manage their bookings. The studio runs the schedule, coaches and payments from an admin dashboard.

Built with React, Express and PostgreSQL.

## Why it is here

It looks like a real business site, photography and all, and it has the admin side that real businesses actually need. Most booking apps stop at the booking.
