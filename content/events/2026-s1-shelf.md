*A final project from Applications Development (Flutter), 2026 first semester. Part of [Seventy-three Flutter apps, and the eight I keep showing people](/calendar/2026-s1-appdev-students).*

## What it is

Shelf keeps room views, storage locations, items, moves and loans on one device, so whoever is on duty can find the thing and put it back where it belongs. Sign-in is Google through Supabase, with a six-digit passcode for coming back.

Built with Flutter, Dart and Supabase Auth.

## Why it is here

There is a full design system in the repo, color through accessibility to a handoff checklist, done in a loud neo-brutalist lime that I would not have dared. Then the app actually follows it. That second part is rarer than the first.
