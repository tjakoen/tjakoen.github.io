*A final project from Full-stack JavaScript, 2026 first semester. Part of [Fifteen full-stack apps from a semester I almost did not survive](/calendar/2026-s1-apsi-students).*

## What it is

A tactical card survival game with deck building, a collection, and a battle board, built as a non-commercial fan project. The artwork belongs to CAPCOM and the music to CAPCOM and CD PROJEKT RED, as the README says up front.

Built with React.

## Why it is here

The most cinematic interface I saw all semester. It is a fan project on someone else's art, credited as such, so it lives here as a look at the build, not the assets.
