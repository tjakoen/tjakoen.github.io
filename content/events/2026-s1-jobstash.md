## What it is

JobStash is a digital clipboard for job hunting. Save a posting link the moment you see it, then open the same list on your laptop to actually apply, track where each application stands, and see where your leads come from.

Built with React, Express and PostgreSQL.

## Why it is here

It has the best one-line pitch in the cohort, and the app keeps the promise the pitch makes. Clean landing page, a square that reads like an ad, and a demo mode so you can try it without signing up.
