*A final project from Full-stack JavaScript, 2026 first semester. Part of [Fifteen full-stack apps from a semester I almost did not survive](/calendar/2026-s1-apsi-students).*

## What it is

LaundryLog follows an order from drop-off to pickup, assigns machine loads, records payments, and keeps soap and add-on inventory tied to the orders that use them.

Built with React, Express and PostgreSQL.

## Why it is here

It understands the shop. Machines have capacities, loads get assigned, and payments come in pieces, all the unglamorous details a real owner would check first. These screenshots are from the project's own docs.
