*A final project from Full-stack JavaScript, 2026 first semester. Part of [Fifteen full-stack apps from a semester I almost did not survive](/calendar/2026-s1-apsi-students).*

## What it is

Plan a shop from a starter catalog or your own items, check things off in the aisle, and finish the trip without typing in a single price. Estimated totals, actual totals and a budget are there if you want them, and out of your way if you do not.

Built with React, Express and PostgreSQL.

## Why it is here

It lives on its own domain, with product, design and operations docs to match. That is someone treating a final project as a product, not a submission.
