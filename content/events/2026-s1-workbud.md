## What it is

WorkBud logs each day of on-the-job training, keeps the running total of hours the school actually asks for, and adds up what the placement is costing you in fares and food along the way.

Built with React, Express and Supabase.

## Why it is here

Every one of their classmates is about to need this, which is the best kind of problem. The README is candid about how Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini were used and where they got it wrong. That is the sentence I wish more professionals would write.
