## What it is

Remember where you ate, what you ordered and how it tasted, with your own photos from each visit. Keep entries private, or share reviews and follow other diners' journals.

Built with React, Express and PostgreSQL.

## Why it is here

The most playful branding in its section, and an AI-usage note that says plainly which parts Claude Code and Codex wrote and which parts are theirs. That is how you fly the flag.
