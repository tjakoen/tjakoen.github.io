## What it is

Life-Quest is a productivity site dressed as an RPG. Add a quest, pick a category and an XP reward, build habit streaks, and earn badges in the reward hall.

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Why it is here

The look commits completely, gold serif and all. The team also published how much of it AI wrote, right at the top of the README, which is exactly the kind of honesty the course asks for.
