*A final project from Applications Development (Flutter), 2026 first semester. Part of [Seventy-three Flutter apps, and the eight I keep showing people](/calendar/2026-s1-appdev-students).*

## What it is

Bahantabay lets commuters save their usual routes, file public flood reports with depth and road status, and see whether a route crosses an active report. Guests can read reports and try demo routes without an account.

Built with Flutter and Dart.

## Why it is here

Of every idea in both sections, this is the one that could matter on a bad day in August. It is local in the way I keep asking for: it knows the streets around campus by name.
