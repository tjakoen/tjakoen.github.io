## What it is

Join a circle and you share its calendar: who is doing what, when, and where they will be. It works for a barkada, a class, or a team at work.

Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## Why it is here

It reads like students made it, in the best sense: jokes in the code comments, honest notes about what is unfinished, and a feature (seeing when everyone is free) that they clearly wanted for themselves.
