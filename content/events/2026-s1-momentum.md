## What it is

Momentum pulls your calendars in from a published ICS link, puts your tasks and events on one dashboard, runs a focus timer against them, and shows you afterwards where the hours actually went.

Built with Flutter, Dart and Cloudflare Workers.

## Why it is here

It is big, and it is tested. A lot of student apps are wide and shallow; this one goes deep on the boring parts, like calendar import, that make a planner usable instead of decorative.
