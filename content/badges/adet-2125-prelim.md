## What this badge attests

Dart and object-oriented programming, then Flutter widgets, layout and state, building an interactive mobile UI. Covers modules 1 to 3.

## Technologies

Dart, object-oriented programming, Flutter widgets, layout and state.

## Where it sits

Application Development and Emerging Technologies carries two badges. Its prelim badge, Dart and Flutter Foundations, covers Dart and Flutter foundations for an interactive mobile UI. Its midterm badge, Flutter App Development, covers stateful, multi-screen Flutter applications. This is the prelim badge.

## How it is earned

The holder was an active student in the prelim block of Application Development and Emerging Technologies (6ADET), section 2125, and completed real coursework in it. The badge is awarded on completion, meaning submitted work with content rather than a grade cutoff. Each recipient's award is a separate, verifiable credential opened from their own badge link. This page states the criteria every recipient met, not a list of who met them.
