Welcome to my portfolio, a small desk you can poke at. Three short cards show where the light and
dark switch, the flavor and the full tour live. Close this any time.

## dark-mode
- verify: Click the highlighted button three times; it should go light, dark, then match the device, with its icon and tooltip following, and a reload should keep the choice.
This is where the choice you just made lives. Each click moves it one step: light, dark, then
match your device, which follows your system setting as it changes. Whatever you land on is saved
in this browser.

## flavor
- verify: Click the flavor name; it should cycle through sourdough, baguette and brioche, and the label should follow.
This one cycles the flavor. Brioche is the default, and sourdough and baguette are the other two.

## guided-tour
- verify: Click Tour; the longer walk through the desk should start from its first card.
When you want the longer walk through the desk, it lives here in the dock. Start it whenever you
like.
