This site opens in brioche, the light flavor, the first time you visit. Two controls are worth knowing
before you start reading, and this walk shows you both. It runs once, and you can close it at any
point.

## dark-mode
- verify: Click the highlighted button; the page should switch between light and dark, and reloading should keep your choice.
This button switches the page between light and dark. Your choice is saved in this browser, so the
site opens the way you left it next time.

## flavor
- verify: Click the flavor name; it should cycle through sourdough, baguette and brioche, and the label should follow.
This one cycles the flavor. Brioche is the default, and sourdough and baguette are the other two.

## guided-tour
- verify: Click Tour; the longer walk through the desk should start from its first card.
When you want the longer walk through the desk, it lives here in the dock. Start it whenever you
like.
