This is my portfolio, laid out for a phone. Everything you need sits in the bar at the bottom of
the screen, and this short tour shows what each button does. Close it at any point.

## mobile-menu
- verify: Tap Menu; the rail should slide in with Projects, Notes, Calendar, Mail, Plans and About, and tapping it again should close it.
Menu opens the rail, the same list of sections the desktop keeps on the left: projects, notes, the
calendar, mail, plans and the about page. Tap a section to go there, or tap Menu again to close it.

## mobile-browse
- verify: Tap Browse; the sheet should rise above the bar with section tiles and recent updates, and tapping it again should lower it.
Browse raises a sheet with every section as a tile and the latest updates under them. It is the
quickest way to see what is new without knowing where it lives.

## dark-mode
- verify: Tap the highlighted button; it should move through light, dark and match the device, and the next page should keep the choice.
This switch moves the site through light, dark and matching your phone's setting. Whatever you pick
is saved in this browser.

## mobile-tour
- verify: Tap Tour; this tour should start again from its first card.
Tour brings you back here whenever you want the walk again.
