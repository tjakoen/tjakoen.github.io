Sources

The people whose work my notes build on. Their books, essays and videos have a card on the reading page.

  1. AO

    Addy OsmaniEngineer and author

    Addy gave me the words for what I was already fumbling toward with AI: durable work is a loop built from a few reusable parts, not one clever prompt. His seventy percent problem is the honest half of that. The machine gets you most of the way, and the last stretch is where unmanaged work rots. Every gate and handoff in my setup exists because of that last stretch. His comprehension-debt warning is the reason the report and the checklist are in the loop at all.

    Find them onWebsiteGitHub

    His workLoop EngineeringBeyond Vibe Coding

  2. AG

    Alfonso GrazianoEngineer and author

    Alfonso's book is where most of my thinking on handing work to an AI got sharpened. Give a model one sentence of instruction and watch what it does first. If it guesses the stack or invents a folder, that gap is mine and not the model's, and the fix is to write down what I meant. The front door in the setup guide is that fix.

    Find them onWebsiteGitHub

    His workLearning AI-Native Software Engineering
