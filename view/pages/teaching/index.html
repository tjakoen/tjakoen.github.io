How the work is graded

Every course runs on a GitHub-native platform I built. Students submit real repositories; each activity is graded against a canonical test suite, and design work carries a written rubric. A badge is earned on completion of a term's activities, meaning a non-zero score on every core piece of work, not a grade cutoff. The badge attests that the holder did the work, and the roster on each badge page links to the repositories where they did it.
